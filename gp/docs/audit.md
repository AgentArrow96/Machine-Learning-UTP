# Independent audit of the FloodNet pipeline and results

Generated by `audit/run_audit.py`. Checks graded: 28 PASS, 2 FAIL (D6b, V3), 2 reported without a threshold. Criteria were fixed before running in [audit/evals.md](../audit/evals.md).

## Bottom line

- **Independent recomputation:** 18/18 reported test scores equal the score recomputed by code that never imports the project package, starting from the raw release files; 18/18 runs give pixel-identical predictions and identical confusion matrices (6/6 U-Net, 6/6 U-Net + CRF, 6/6 RF).
- **Retraining from scratch:** 12/12 retrained models are bitwise identical to the frozen ones (U-Net: every weight tensor; RF: every region prediction). Largest score difference over all 18 runs after retraining: 0.0000 points.
- **Scores depend on the true labels:** scoring each model against another image's mask gives at most 1.31% flooded mIoU; untrained networks reach at most 1.16%; the weakest trained model scores 22.28%.
- **Test isolation:** instrumented retraining logged every image ID given to training and validation; test IDs touched = 0. 18/18 test evaluations happened after their freeze.
- **Not a pipeline bug, but a real limit:** the official split itself puts overlapping drone frames in training and test (357/448 test images; 49/52 with flooded targets). Randomly drawn verified pairs are shown in `output/audit/figures/overlap_pairs_random.png`. Scores describe already-seen areas, not a new flood.

## How the audit was done

- **Skills followed** (cloned read-only to `C:\Important Files\audit-skills\`, push disabled, outside every working folder): Superpowers `verification-before-completion` (no claim without fresh evidence) and `systematic-debugging` (root cause before any change); ECC `eval-harness` (evals defined first, code graders, pass^k), `verification-loop` (build/types/lint/tests/security/diff report), `mle-workflow` and the `mle-reviewer` checklist.
- **Independence:** `audit/independent.py`, `audit_data.py`, `audit_eval.py`, `audit_stats.py`, `audit_code.py` and `make_audit_figures.py` never import `floodnet` (each asserts it at the end). They re-derive inputs from the raw JPEG/PNG files, rebuild models from checkpoints with `segmentation_models_pytorch`, `joblib` and `pydensecrf`, and score with `sklearn.metrics.confusion_matrix`. Only `audit_retrain.py` imports the project, to retrain with its own code.
- **Repetition:** data, evaluation and statistics checks ran twice; a check passes only if both runs pass and produce identical results.
- **Rerun it yourself:** `Set-Location 'C:\Important Files\ML\gp'; .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .\audit\run_audit.py` (≈ 1 hour; retrains all 12 models into `.tmp/`).

## Root-cause log (Superpowers systematic-debugging)

- **D5, first run FAILED** (pixel agreement 79.4%; IDs 0 and 7 mapped to unexpected colours). Reproduced on single images: test_10163 (4000×3000): stretched 100.0%, top-left 4000×3000 window 100.0%; test_6336 (4000×3000): stretched 100.0%, top-left 4000×3000 window 100.0%; test_7486 (4592×3072): stretched 64.1%, top-left 4000×3000 window 100.0%. Root cause: the official colour masks render the top-left 4000×3000 window, so the 4592×3072 masks had been compared after a wrong stretch. With the corrected comparison: pixel agreement 99.46% (4592x3072: mean 97.54%, min 6.58% over 77 masks, 4000x3000: mean 99.82%, min 86.61% over 73 masks). The indexed labels the pipeline uses were not affected. The criterion was kept, and D6b was added to check every photo against its full mask.

- **D5, remaining disagreement** (still a PASS overall): 21 of 352 large-photo colour masks agree below 99%. At the 3-class level used by this project, 20 of 21 agree 100%: the colour masks draw background in an off-palette blue (0, 163, 255), a rendering difference only. One image differs in a target class: train_6600 (background->road-flooded 322,761 px at 1024 px; in the 25% budget: True). The indexed mask (used) labels that region background; the photo shows a dry baseball infield, so the indexed label is the plausible one. It affects one training image and no validation or test image. Evidence: `figures/d5_low_agreement_examples.png`, `d5_three_class_check.json`.

- **D6b FAILED its pre-set criterion** (1927/2103 photos score higher with their own mask; 240 single-class masks have no boundary to test). Investigation of the 176 non-passing photos: 105 score best with a flipped/rotated mask, but the preferred transform is inconsistent (rot180 48, fliplr 40, flipud 17), all are EXIF orientation 1, and only 2 contain any flooded pixel. Visual check of the strongest cases (`figures/d6b_orientation_candidates.png`): the published masks are correctly aligned and every flipped version is wrong. Root cause: the edge-agreement metric is too weak on near-uniform vegetation scenes with few label boundaries, where another image's mask can score higher by chance. No data defect found; the FAIL is kept as recorded.

- **V3 FAILED** (10 ruff bug-rule findings, {'F401': 5, 'F541': 1, 'F821': 4}). Inspected: the F821 items are a `del model, x, y, loss` inside the out-of-memory fallback of `unet_preflight` (src/floodnet/training.py), guarded by `except UnboundLocalError` and never executed (the recorded preflight succeeded at batch 4). The rest are unused imports and one placeholder-free f-string. None changes a result; the project code was left unchanged, as the reviewer rule requires.

## What this audit does not prove

- That the FloodNet annotations themselves are correct (only that they are used as published and mean what the release says).
- Performance on a new flood event or an area not seen in training (the official split cannot measure this).
- Statistical significance across training runs beyond three seeds (the bootstrap covers test-image sampling, not training randomness).
